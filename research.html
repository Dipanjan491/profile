01 — Doctoral Thesis
Ph.D. in Psychometry · Christ University

Construction & Validation of a Moral Intelligence Scale for the Indian Context.

Moral intelligence cannot be imported uncritically across cultural paradigms. This doctoral research constructs and calibrates a multi-dimensional measurement model attuned to social complexities, cultural norms, and contextual dilemmas in India.

By applying Item Response Theory (IRT) alongside Classical Test Theory and Latent Profile Analysis (LPA), the scale achieves high precision across both typical and extreme trait ranges while detecting sub-population moral profiles.
