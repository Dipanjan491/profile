01 — Consulting Services
Instrument Design

Scale Construction

End-to-end development of psychometric scales tailored to specific cultural, organizational, or clinical contexts. Item pool generation, cognitive interviewing, pilot administration, and refinement.

Psychometric Rigor

Construct Validation

Factorial validity via Exploratory & Confirmatory Factor Analysis (EFA/CFA), structural equation modeling (SEM), convergent/discriminant validity tests, and multi-group measurement invariance.

Modern Test Theory

IRT & Item Calibration

Item parameter estimation (discrimination, difficulty, guessing), Item Information Curves (IIC), Test Information Functions (TIF), and Graded Response Models (GRM) for polytomous items.

Typology & Segmentation

Latent Profile Analysis

Identification of distinct unobserved behavioral, cognitive, or moral profiles across heterogeneous groups using Gaussian mixture models and latent class diagnostics.

Fairness & Precision

Bias & DIF Analytics

Differential Item Functioning (DIF) across demographic, linguistic, or cultural strata. Ensuring items perform equitably without systematic measurement bias.

Capacity Building

Workshops & Mentorship

Training post-graduate researchers, faculty, and industry teams in computational psychometrics with R, Jamovi, and reproducible research workflows.
