01 — The Stance

Curiosity without presumption.

As a clinician with intensive M.Phil training from the University of Calcutta and seven years in hospital, university, and community settings, I do not believe one school of psychotherapy holds all the answers.

Some suffering calls for the clarity and cognitive reframing of CBT: identifying automatic negative patterns, restructuring cognitive distortions, and reclaiming agency over behavioral habits.

Other pain stems from deeply patterned relational templates, early attachments, and unconscious defenses that require the patient slowness of psychodynamic exploration.

Still other moments demand experiential attunement to emotional regulation — allowing feelings to be felt, understood, and transformed through Emotion Focused Therapy (EFT).

02 — Modalities
Evidence-Based Structure

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)

Focuses on the interplay between thoughts, emotional states, and physiological responses. Highly effective for anxiety disorders, panic, obsessive ruminations, and depressive episodes.

Depth & Relational Insight

Psychodynamic Psychotherapy

Uncovers unconscious conflicts, recurring relational patterns, and internalized self-narratives. Illuminates how past experiences shape present choices and emotional blockages.

Affective Processing

Emotion Focused Therapy (EFT)

Treats emotions as a compass for authentic needs rather than symptoms to suppress. Helps integrate unresolved grief, identity crises, and emotional vulnerability.
